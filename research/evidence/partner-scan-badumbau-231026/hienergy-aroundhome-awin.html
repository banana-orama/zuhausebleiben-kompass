About Aroundhome DE

Aroundhome is a leading German online platform that connects homeowners with verified local service professionals for home improvement and maintenance projects. The platform offers a comprehensive marketplace for services including roofing, heating, solar installation, windows, doors, flooring, bathrooms, kitchens, and garden landscaping. Users can request free, non-binding quotes from pre-vetted professionals in their area, compare offers, and read authentic customer reviews. Aroundhome handles the entire process from initial inquiry to project completion, ensuring quality and reliability. The platform serves as a trusted intermediary that simplifies finding qualified tradespeople while providing professionals with qualified leads. With a focus on transparency and customer satisfaction, Aroundhome has become one of Germany's most popular home service marketplaces.

Program details

Aroundhome's affiliate program on Awin offers commissions for referring customers who request quotes for home services. Affiliates earn revenue when users complete quote requests through their referral links.

Company history

Founded in 2008 as MyHammer, the platform rebranded to Aroundhome in 2021 to reflect its expanded focus beyond just craftsmen services to comprehensive home solutions. The company has grown to become one of Germany's largest online home service marketplaces, connecting millions of homeowners with qualified professionals across various home improvement categories.
