 Opt-out and data erasure 
 Triple A from Artefact Germany GmbH is based on the easy.affiliate software from EASY Marketing GmbH and uses cookies to ensure conversion and customer journey tracking for billing partnerships. These cookies are used to anonymously store data on touchpoints. These touchpoints are created by the insertion of a banner, a click on a banner of a partner website, optionally a site marker (transaction data on the operator side) and the conversion relevant to remuneration.  
 Further information can be found here:  https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion-Tracking . 
   
 The following cookies are set by TripleA / easy.affiliate: 
 TRS: 
 Unique, 24-digit identifier (ID) for tracking partnerships. This cookie is stored in the client browser and linked to database entries on the server side that contain the Touchpoint data. The associated data is stored on servers of EASY Marketing GmbH. 
 TRSCJ: 
 Fallback cookie with the rudimentary Touchpoint data for tracking partnerships. This cookie contains all Touchpoint data in encrypted form and is only stored in your browser. 
   
 Opt-out and deletion (according to Art. 17 DSGVO): 
 If you do not agree with the storage of these cookies, you can refuse and delete the cookies via our opt-out. In this case, all data stored on the ID present in the cookie will be deleted automatically.  
   
 By this deletion, EASY Marketing GmbH as the technical operator and Artefact Germany GmbH as the content operator of this platform comply with Article 17 of the Data Protection Basic Regulation (DSGVO).
